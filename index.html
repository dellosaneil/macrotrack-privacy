FitMetric Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

FitMetric: Macro & Weight Log (“FitMetric” or “the app”), previously called MacroTrack, helps you track calories, macros, weight, body measurements and progress photos. It is made by TheLazyBattley (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. The app has no accounts and no ads, we never ask for your name, email address or phone number, and everything you log stays on your phone.

Information stored only on your device

  • Your profile: age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal and target weight, used to work out your calorie and macro targets.
  • What you log: your food log, weigh-ins and body measurements.
  • Your foods and recipes: foods you create or scan, recipes, favorites and your nutrition goal history.
  • Progress photos: photos you take in the app or add from your photo library, the albums you put them in, and your photo reminder settings.
  • Settings: such as your units and whether you have seen the app tour.

This information is stored in a database on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. You can erase all of it at any time with Profile › Delete all data, and uninstalling the app deletes it too.

Progress photos are kept in the app’s private storage on your phone and are never uploaded. When you add a photo from your photo library, the app saves its own copy and the original stays where it was. Photos are excluded from Android’s cloud backup and from the app’s backup files.

Information collected automatically

The app uses Google Firebase services, which collect some information automatically:

  • Firebase Analytics: how the app is used, such as which screens are opened and that a food or weight was logged, together with an app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address. Analytics events never include the names of foods, amounts, weights, measurements or anything else you type. We use it to understand which features are used and to improve the app.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: when the app crashes or hits an error, a report with technical details of the problem, device model, operating system version, app version and a crash-reporting identifier. We use it only to find and fix bugs.

You can turn both off at any time with Profile › Share crash reports & usage. This information is not used to identify you, and we do not combine it with other data about you.

Services the app connects to

  • Open Food Facts: when you scan or enter a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free, open database of food products, to look up the product’s name and nutrition. Nothing else about you is sent. Its privacy policy is at world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.
  • Camera: the app asks for camera access to scan barcodes and to take progress photos. Barcode scanning is done on your phone by Google’s ML Kit; the camera image is not saved or sent anywhere. Progress photos are saved only on your phone, as described above. To help you line up a new photo with your last one, ML Kit finds the outline of the person in the photo, also on your phone. ML Kit may send Google anonymous information about how these features perform, but never the images.
  • Photo library: the app only sees the photos you choose to add, through your phone’s photo picker.
  • Google Play: if you buy FitMetric Pro, the purchase is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the app only learns whether Pro has been purchased. The app may also show Google Play’s rating prompt, which Google runs.

Backups and exports

When you back up or export your data, the app creates a file and you choose where to save or share it, for example your phone’s storage or Google Drive. The file goes only where you put it, and that service’s own privacy policy then applies. Backup files do not include progress photos. We never receive your backups.

Notifications

If you turn on photo reminders, the app schedules them on your phone. No information is sent to us to deliver them, and you can turn them off in the app or in your phone’s settings.

How we share information

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. Google processes the analytics and crash information described above on our behalf as the provider of Firebase. You can read how Google handles this data at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep information

  • Analytics data: kept for 2 months.
  • Crash reports: kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Data on your device: kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Children

FitMetric is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Your logs stay on your phone and are protected by your phone’s own security. Information sent to Firebase and Open Food Facts is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep what leaves your phone to the minimum described here.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off crash reports and usage data in Profile › Share crash reports & usage.
  • Erase everything on your phone with Profile › Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Choose not to scan barcodes or take photos, or deny camera access; you can still add foods by hand.
  • Delete any progress photo or album in the app at any time.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 or the EU General Data Protection Regulation, including the right to access or delete your information. Contact us to make a request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and post the new version at this address. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s store listing.
